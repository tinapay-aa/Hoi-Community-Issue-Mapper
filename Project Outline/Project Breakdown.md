# Hoi! - Community Complaint Mapper

## Main Features:
- Users can issue a complaint (e.g., broken roads, piling up trash, etc.) and pin their location to bring notice to their issues. 
- Users can mark a complaint as 'done/satisfied' but will be required to attach a picture as proof
- Issues will be mapped.
